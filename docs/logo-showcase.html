The Driga Mark: Three Reins, One Verdict.

Driga draws from TWO converging roots: the classical triga / quadriga (a multi-steed chariot guided by unified reins) and Old Norse drífa / draga (to drive, draw together, or harness traces). Just as a charioteer holds the reins of multiple horses running in parallel across worktrees, Driga gathers telemetry from Claude, Codex, Copilot, Gemini, and Antigravity into a single honest focal point: what is working, and what needs you.
