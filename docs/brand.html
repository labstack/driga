Use with care

Give it space.

Leave clear space around the mark of at least one quarter of its displayed size. Keep the five forms together and preserve the open center.

Keep the silhouette.

Use the supplied SVGs for sharp rendering at any size. Scale proportionally, retain the orientation, and use the white version on dark surfaces.

Choose the right format.

Use the horizontal lockup where the name needs to lead. Use the symbol or app tile in compact spaces. The supplied favicon handles browser tabs.

Editable source

Edit murmur.svg for the five symbol shapes, or wordmark.svg for the lettering. Run python3 scripts/generate-brand.py to regenerate the variants and PNG exports. PNG rendering uses rsvg-convert; --svg-only generates just the vector variants. The master files are preserved. The selected geometry was traced from the approved concept artwork.
